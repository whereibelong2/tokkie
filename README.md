# tokkie 🐰

### tiny receipts for long conversations

*A small experiment in turning all that yapping into something you can actually hold onto. View interactive prototype [here](https://whereibelong2.github.io/tokkie/).*

</div>

---

### what is tokkie? ૮ ˶ᵔ ᵕ ᵔ˶ ა

Long conversations are strange things.

You can spend hours talking to someone — hundreds of messages, thousands of words, recurring jokes, oddly specific topics — and at the end, all of it still looks like... a very long chat log.

Tokkie started with a tiny question:

**what if a conversation could leave you a receipt?**

Instead of trying to summarise everything that was said, Tokkie turns the shape of a conversation into a small keepsake: how much you talked, what kept coming up, the things you always say, and the general mood you left behind.

---

### why a receipt? ₍ᐢ. .ᐢ₎

Receipts are good at making something big feel strangely tangible.

A whole transaction gets compressed into one little piece of paper: what happened, how much of it there was, and a few numbers that somehow make the whole thing feel real.

Tokkie borrows that idea for conversations.

The receipt can show things like:

* total messages and characters
* estimated tokens
* conversation balance
* playful equivalents for how much you talked
* recurring topics
* signature expressions
* the overall mood

Not because every conversation needs to become data — but because sometimes the numbers make the memory a little more fun to look back on.

---

### the prototype ✿

Tokkie currently exists as an **interactive front-end prototype** of the receipt experience.

The prototype explores the visual language of a physical receipt — paper texture, scalloped edges, compact information hierarchy — while adding small interactions that make the output feel less static.

Some details can change or be explored directly, including conversation equivalents and little sign-offs at the bottom of the receipt.

The current data is illustrative rather than generated from an uploaded conversation.

**Conversation import, analysis, and dynamic receipt generation have not been implemented yet.**

---

### what I'm exploring next ᶻ 𝗓 𐰁

The next step is figuring out how Tokkie gets from an actual conversation to the receipt:

**conversation → analysis → little receipt**

That means exploring how to extract useful patterns without turning something personal into an overly serious analytics dashboard — and deciding what information actually deserves a place on the final receipt.

For now, Tokkie is mostly interested in getting the feeling right first.
